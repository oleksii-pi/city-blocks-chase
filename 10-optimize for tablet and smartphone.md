**Optimization of Controls for Tablets and Smartphones:**

1. **Control Design:**
   - Replace the arrows in the bottom right corner with a joystick-style control.
   - Implement a large static circle with a radius of **3R**.
   - Include a smaller movable circle with a radius of **R** that can be operated by touch.

2. **Directional and Speed Control:**
   - When the inner circle is moved to the top position of the joystick, the vehicle will smoothly change direction to face north, reaching maximum speed.
   - Moving the inner circle to the right will change the vehicle's direction to east, with speed proportional to the inner circle's position relative to the center of the large circle.

3. **Minimap Adjustment:**
   - On the iPhone 15, the minimap is currently too large, obscuring the view of the vehicle.
   - Reduce the size of the minimap for smartphones to ensure better visibility of the car and surrounding space.
