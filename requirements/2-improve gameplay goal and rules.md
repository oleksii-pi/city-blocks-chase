**Game Objective:**
The goal of the game is to survive on the streets.

**Chasing Mechanics:**

- If your car collides with another vehicle, the nearest police will begin to chase you.
- If you hit another car again, an additional police unit will join the chase, and this continues with each additional collision.
- If you hit a car, the police will continue to pursue you regardless of the distance, even if it's more than 7 blocks.
- However, the police will only forget about your pursuit if you get within 3 blocks of them and then exceed a distance of 7 blocks again.

**Police Behavior:**

- Police will pursue you using both lanes of the road, and all other vehicles will yield to them.
- The police can only capture you by hitting your car.

**Game Over:**

- If the police successfully collide with your car, a large splash screen will display "Game Over!" with white text on a dimmed city background. This will last for 2 seconds, followed by a gradual darkening animation.
- After this, the game will restart from the beginning.
